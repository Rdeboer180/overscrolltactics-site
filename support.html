PlayDraft support

One inbox, read by a person

Something broke

Email rdeboer@overscrolltactics.com with what happened, your device model, and your iOS version — that's usually enough to reproduce it. A person reads every message and answers.

Joining a friend's draft

Your host shares an invite link or code from the waiting room. Tap the link, or open Drafts in PlayDraft, tap Join with a code, and enter it. Every player, including the host, uses one Draft Ticket. It's held when you join and comes back if you leave before the draft starts or the room doesn't fill within 7 days.

A draft is stuck waiting

A room starts when its seats fill and the countdown ends, or when the host starts it. If a drafter misses their turn, the app picks for them, so one absent player never stalls the board. If the room doesn't fill within 7 days, everyone's Draft Ticket comes back.

Reporting a message or a player

Press and hold any message in a draft room or conversation to report or block its author. Reports reach a moderator, and blocking is immediate. Anything urgent can also come straight to the address above.

Deleting your account

Tap your avatar at the top right, then All settings, then Delete account. Deletion is permanent and immediate: it removes your profile and your personal data. Picks and messages already in a shared draft stay as that group's history with your name detached from them.

Everything else

Press, partnerships, and questions about the studio go to the same address. rdeboer@overscrolltactics.com reaches OverscrollTactics LLC directly.
