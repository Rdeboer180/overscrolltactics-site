PlayDraft support

One inbox, read by a person

Something broke

Email rdeboer@overscrolltactics.com with what happened, your device model, and your iOS version — that's usually enough to reproduce it. A person reads every message and answers.

Joining a friend's draft

Your host shares an invite code from the waiting room. In PlayDraft, open Find a draft, tap Have an invite code?, and enter it. A write-in Custom Draft is free to join — the host covers it with a Host Ticket. A pack draft spends a Draft Ticket for each drafter, including the host.

A draft is stuck waiting

A room starts once its seats fill. If a drafter misses their turn the clock hands their pick to autodraft, so one absent player never stalls the board. Leave a Competitive lobby that never fills and your entry comes straight back.

Reporting a message or a player

Press and hold any message in a draft room, league, or direct message to report or block its author. Reports reach a moderator, and blocking is immediate. Anything urgent can also come straight to the address above.

Deleting your account

Open the profile menu, then Settings, then Delete Account. Deletion is permanent and immediate: it removes your profile, your collection, and your personal data. Picks and messages already in a shared draft stay as that group's history with your name detached from them.

Everything else

Press, partnerships, and questions about the studio go to the same address. rdeboer@overscrolltactics.com reaches OverscrollTactics LLC directly.
