PlayDraft

A social drafting game for iPhone. Pick a themed pack, draft your lineup against friends, and let the group vote for the winner.

  • Live drafts

    Head-to-head picks from themed packs — movies, music, food, and beyond.

  • Group voting

    Finished lineups go to the group. The vote decides the winner — no arguing with the scoreboard.

  • Streaks & standings

    Daily packs and running standings keep the rivalry going between drafts.
