From spreadsheet to draft room.

Keep the header row exactly as it is. Add one draftable choice per row.

  1. Fill the sheet.Asset title is required. Descriptor and Category are optional. Each row can have one category label; use at most three different labels across the board.
  2. Export a CSV.Save or export as CSV with the columns in this order: Asset title, Descriptor, Category. Keep the file in Files, iCloud Drive, or another location available to your iPhone.
  3. Import in PlayDraft.Open Packs → Custom Draft → Build a Draftboard → Import a CSV. Review the choices, fix any flagged rows, then tap Use this board before you create the room.
How many choices?

Add at least seats × picks per person. A room with 3 drafters and 5 picks each needs 15 choices. Boards support up to 500 choices. Category labels add color; they do not require anyone to draft from each category.

No spreadsheet? You can type or paste choices directly in the app. Need a hand? Visit PlayDraft support.
