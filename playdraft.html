The idea

Your argument.
Now it's a draft.

Everybody has the argument. Best candy. Greatest sitcom. Who'd actually win with invisibility. PlayDraft turns it into a real draft. On the clock, snake order, no take-backs.

No season to commit to and no spreadsheet. Draft in one sitting on a 2-minute clock, or let it run all week alongside your group chat.

How a draft plays

Four steps from "I've got a take" to a recap in the group chat.

  1. Pick a pack

    Choose from fourteen free packs, from Candy to GOAT Athletes, or make your own in Custom.

  2. Invite your friends

    Every draft is a private room for 3 to 8 players. Share the invite link or code, and the host can start once everyone's in.

  3. Draft on the clock

    Snake-order picks off a live board. Search or filter the pack, queue your targets, and talk trash in the room chat.

  4. Compare lineups

    The recap shows every lineup and the full pick order. Share it as an image or as text, then draft again.

Set the rules

The host sets up the room before anyone picks. Everything here is a real setting in the app.

Players
3 to 8 drafters in a private room. Invite them with a link or a join code.
Picks each
3 to 8 picks per player from a ready-made pack, or 3 to 7 from a pack you made.
Pick clock
2 minutes to draft together, or 8 hours to pick when you can.
Pick order
Snake order. The order reverses every round, so the last seat picks back-to-back. It's drawn when the draft starts.
Starting
The draft starts when the room fills and the countdown ends, or the host starts it right away.
Missed picks
Miss your pick and the app picks for you, so one slow friend never stalls the room.

Make your own pack

Name a topic, add the choices, and draft it with your group.

  • Add your choices

    Up to 500

    Type them in, paste a list with one choice per line, or import a spreadsheet as a CSV. Add an optional descriptor and category label to any choice.

  • Save it in Custom

    Your library

    Your packs live in the Custom tab. Save a list now and finish it later, then host a draft from it whenever your group is ready.

  • Start from the template

    Free CSV

    The PlayDraft CSV template has the columns the importer expects, plus every rule it checks.

Draft Tickets

One ticket.
One draft.

Each player uses one Draft Ticket per draft. It's held when you join and returned if you leave before the draft starts or the room doesn't fill within 7 days.

New players start with 6. Free to play, no subscriptions.
